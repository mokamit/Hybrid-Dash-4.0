Hybrid-Dash 4.0 Quick Start Beta

From zero to your first race in about five minutes.

Open the app Source on GitHub

Before you start

  • Browser: Chrome or Edge on Windows, Android or ChromeOS. Safari and Firefox cannot use Bluetooth, so they can only run the app without cars.
  • Cars: Carrera Hybrid cars with charged batteries and Bluetooth on. One car is enough to start.
  • Controller: optional. PlayStation and Xbox pads work; so does the keyboard. Connect the pad by Bluetooth or USB and press any button once so the browser notices it.
  • No car or track? You can still look around, and in the Garage Drive virtually (beta) lets you drive without a car.
This is a beta. Things can break. Settings are saved in your browser, so use the same browser each time.

1Connect your car

  • On the main menu, press Connect on the Cars & Drivers card.
  • Pick your car in the browser's Bluetooth window. Repeat for every car (the browser asks once per car).
  • Garage lists your cars. Give each one a role with the arrows: Player 1 to 8, NPC (computer driver) or Safety car. Name, colour and number are remembered.
  • Start NPCs in the Garage adds computer drivers. Reconnect brings back the cars you used last time with one click.

2Choose a track

  • Scan: the car drives your real track and the app maps it piece by piece.
  • Editor: build or adjust the track on screen. Load opens one you saved.
  • NPCs, the racing line, sectors and most 4.0 features need a track.

3Set up and start the race

  • On the Start Race card, the arrows on the Race Options bar switch the race type. Click the bar for duration, weather and mandatory pit stops.
  • Press the red Start Race bar (Enter, or A / Cross on a pad).
  • You see the grid, then the start lights, then it is on. Esc or B goes back in the menus.

Local multiplayer in 30 seconds

  • Pair each controller with the laptop and press any button on it.
  • Open Multiplayer → Local players and press a button on each controller to join.
  • In the Garage give each connected car the matching Player role, then start the race.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth multiplayer between several devices is on the same page, above the local players.

Two laptops, more cars

One laptop holds roughly 7 to 9 Bluetooth cars. For bigger fields open helper.html on a second laptop on the same Wi-Fi, create a pairing code on the first (Garage → Two laptops) and type it on the second.

If something does not work

  • Car does not show up: check the car is on and not connected to another phone or laptop, then try Connect again.
  • Pad not detected: press a button on it once. Browsers only show a controller after its first input.
  • Old version showing: reload the page. If it still looks old, clear the site data for this page.
  • Everything feels wrong: Options has presets (Arcade, Pro, GT3 and more) for the driving feel.
